Profile view tracking ("Duke coach viewed your profile 3× today"). Needs a new profile_views table + RLS.

Activity feed: new matches, upcoming league events from league_events, film count nudges.

Later — second pass

Film view count ("your film was watched 7× this week"). Needs a view counter column + increment trigger.

HOT badge on coach cards (highest match % gets it). "lanista" wordmark. TikTok nav with Film as center FAB.
